About

Umeed-e-Sahar is a student-run publication exploring the ideas, events and forces that shape the world around us.

We write about history, economics, society and culture with one simple aim: to make complicated things understandable and familiar things worth looking at again.

No shouting. No rush. Just thoughtful writing, careful research, and the occasional question that stays with you.
