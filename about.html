There is always more to notice.

Umeed-e-Sahar is a student-run publication exploring history, economics, society and culture through a Pakistani lens.

The idea

A quiet wake-up call.

Umeed-e-Sahar began with a simple belief: public understanding does not always need volume. Sometimes a carefully told story can do more than a loud argument.

We want to make difficult subjects familiar and familiar subjects worth looking at again.

Our work is rooted in Pakistan — its languages, streets, institutions, memories and everyday rituals.

Our editorial rules

Curious, careful, human.

  • We separate reporting, interpretation and opinion.
  • We cite sources and correct mistakes.
  • We avoid manufactured outrage.
  • We write for students, but not down to them.
  • We look for the human story inside the large idea.
