Why Umeed-e-Sahar?

We are students trying to understand the world around us — and inviting other students to do the same.

We believe awareness does not need to arrive as a shout.

Sometimes it arrives as a question. A paragraph. A poem. A piece of history you had forgotten. A number you finally understand.

Umeed-e-Sahar is our attempt to make those moments of understanding linger.
