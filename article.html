The Things We Stop Asking

Curiosity, conformity and the courage to ask better questions.

UMEED-E-SAHAR · EDITORIAL

A society does not only change through grand events. It changes through the small questions people stop asking: Why is this done this way? Who benefits? What did we forget? Could there be another way?

This is the spirit behind Umeed-e-Sahar. We are students, not an institution pretending to have every answer. Our job is to look closely, read widely and make room for better questions.
